MatchBrief — Arsenal 1-0 Atlético Madrid (2-1 agg) — CL SF 2nd Leg

Arsenal 1-0 Atlético Madrid (2-1 agg) — Champions League Semi-Final, 2nd Leg Arteta went bold. Calafiori at LB pushing inside, Lewis-Skelly in a thrusting central role, Rice sitting deeper. It was a statement team sheet — Arsenal weren't going to wait. Simeone did what Simeone does. Two banks of four. Compact. Disciplined. Daring Arsenal to break them down. For 43 minutes, they couldn't. Three times Arsenal got in behind. Three times Atlético locked the middle. Then Saliba played a ball up the inside-right channel, Gyökeres crossed, Trossard's shot was parried by Oblak — and Saka was sharpest to the rebound. The second half was pure Simeone. Atlético pushed, Arsenal sat in. But for all of Atlético's pressure, they managed 9 shots and 0.53 xG across 90 minutes. Arsenal's defensive structure conceded the ball, not the chances. Sørloth's miss in the 86th minute was the moment. After that, Arsenal saw it out with the kind of professional game management that used to be Simeone's trademark. The tactical story: Arteta's aggressive team selection forced Simeone to adapt. By sitting Rice deeper and pushing both FBs inside, Arsenal created a back three in possession that Atlético's 4-4-2 couldn't press. When Atlético had to chase the game, they had no tools — 0.53 xG over 90 minutes tells you everything.
